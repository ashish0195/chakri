Chakri, the Diwali party game for one phone

Chakri is a free party game for Diwali night. It has two games. Jodi Check is a quiz for two that shows how well you really know each other. Chakri Truth or Dare is for 2 to 12 players, with the phone in the middle of the room. Both play in the browser, in English with a Hinglish switch at the top. There's no app to install and no sign-up, and nothing you type leaves the phone.

How to play Jodi Check

  1. Choose your jodi (a couple, siblings, best friends or family) and type both names.
  2. The first player answers six questions about themselves in secret while the other looks away.
  3. Pass the phone. The second player guesses each answer from the same four options, and every match lights a diya.
  4. Swap for six more questions. Light all 12 diyas to finish the rangoli.

The score earns a title. Twelve out of twelve is Made in the same factory, 10 or 11 is a Kaju katli jodi, 7 to 9 a Gulab jamun jodi and 4 to 6 a Soan papdi jodi, passed around for years and never actually opened. The better guesser wins bragging rights, and you get a story-sized card to share.

How to play Chakri Truth or Dare

  1. Add everyone's names, from 2 to 12 players, and choose your decks.
  2. Swipe the chakri or tap Spin. It sprays sparks and stops on someone. It never picks the same person twice in a row, and anyone who has had fewer turns is likelier to come up.
  3. That player picks a patakha, and a card comes up with their name on it.
  4. Do it for the points, or pay one of your three laddoos to skip it.
  5. When the night is done, tap End game to see the Patakha of the night, the scores and the awards.

Four decks for every crowd

  • Family is safe in front of Dadi, with cards about sweets, Diwali cleaning, outfits and the family photo.
  • Friends shows no mercy, from first crushes to the message sent to the wrong group chat.
  • Couple is a little spicy and only opens when exactly two people play.
  • Aunty Special brings the nosy relatives, live. "Beta, when are you getting married? Just give us the year, we'll book the caterer."

Chakri has 176 truth or dare cards and 82 Jodi Check questions, each in English and Hinglish. The cards stick to the social side of Diwali, like the sweets, the lights, the relatives and the box of soan papdi that does the rounds. No dare involves fire, firecrackers, diyas, drink or smoke, and none sends anyone outside.

Questions

Is Chakri free?

Yes. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

Do we need to download an app?

No. Chakri runs in the phone's web browser. Open chakri.9io.ai on one phone and pass it around.

How many people can play?

Jodi Check is for two players. Chakri Truth or Dare takes 2 to 12, with the phone in the middle.

Is Chakri OK to play with family and kids?

Choose the Family deck, which is written to be safe in front of grandparents. No dare in any deck involves fire, firecrackers, drink or going outside, and the Couple deck only opens when exactly two people play.

Can we play in Hindi?

Tap Hinglish at the top and every question, card and button switches to Hinglish, which mixes Hindi and English in Roman letters.

Does Chakri keep our names or answers?

Only on your phone. The browser remembers your language, the sound setting and the last names you typed, so the next game starts faster. Jodi Check answers aren't saved, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
